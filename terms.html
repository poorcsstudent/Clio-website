Pilot terms

Use ClioVision thoughtfully.

Last updated July 29, 2026. ClioVision is an experimental, independent campus-tour pilot—not a production navigation, safety, admissions, or accessibility service.

Walking safety comes first

Pay attention to your surroundings. Follow posted sidewalks, closures, signs, campus rules, and accessibility guidance. Do not use ClioVision while driving, cycling in traffic, or in any situation where a screen or audio prompt would distract you.

Routes can be wrong or outdated

Tour routes, GPS arrival zones, building facts, and generated answers are in active validation. Verify important information with official campus sources and never rely on the app as your only source of direction or safety information.

Wearables are optional

Connected glasses and Bluetooth audio are optional features. Keep audio at a level that allows you to hear your surroundings, and do not use wearable features where they are prohibited.

Independent project

ClioVision is not affiliated with or endorsed by Missouri University of Science and Technology, Meta, Ray-Ban, or their respective departments. Names and marks belong to their owners.

Pilot availability

Features may change, fail, or be removed during testing. Access is not guaranteed, and the pilot may be limited to certain devices, operating-system versions, routes, or participants.

Feedback

By participating, you may submit feedback about the app or routes. Do not share sensitive personal information. Use the interest form on the main site to request access or share feedback.
