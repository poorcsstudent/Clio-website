Missouri S&T

The Computer Science walking pilot.

ClioVision’s current campus walk is a field-validation experience—not a finished public navigation product. It is designed to test route guidance, arrival narration, questions, and reliable voice recovery.

What a pilot walk tests

Useful at each stop. Calm between them.

Each walk checks whether the route makes sense on foot, whether an arrival is recognized at the right time, and whether the content and audio interaction feel useful in the real environment.

ClioVision is a companion—not a safety or accessibility navigation service. Follow posted routes, campus guidance, and your own judgment while walking.
