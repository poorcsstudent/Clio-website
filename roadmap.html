Development roadmap

A campus-ready guide, built in deliberate stages.

ClioVision is moving from a working Computer Science walking pilot to a reliable, hands-free guide for the whole Missouri S&T campus. The sequence starts with real-world walking validation, then expands tours, mapping, AI, and release readiness.

  1. 01

    August 2026

    Stabilize the pilot

    Field-test routes, GPS arrivals, voice recovery, and glasses connection on real campus walks.

  2. 02

    Aug–Sep 2026

    Expand degree tours

    Curate every undergraduate program’s route, buildings, narration, timing, and field-review status.

  3. 03

    September 2026

    Build a production map

    Add sidewalk-aware mapping, building markers, accessibility choices, and offline campus support.

  4. 04

    Sep–Oct 2026

    Make guidance smarter

    Add rerouting, turn prompts, resume behavior, and hands-free commands such as “what’s next?”

  5. 05

    October 2026

    Grow campus AI

    Ground answers in official sources and make Clio aware of the current tour, stop, and visitor question.

  6. 06

    Oct 2026 onward

    Beta to release

    Harden voice and privacy, recruit testers, ship TestFlight builds, and prepare the public launch.

Next milestone

Walk the Computer Science pilot on campus and use findings to improve arrival zones, route geometry, narration, and Bluetooth recovery before expanding to other majors.

Security, privacy, content review, and route safety are built into every phase.
