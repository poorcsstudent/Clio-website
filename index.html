What is live today

A guided walking pilot for Missouri S&T.

The current pilot focuses on a Computer Science campus walk. It is built to test real route guidance, on-arrival narration, voice questions, and recovery when a walk or audio session is interrupted.

ClioVision is a companion—not a safety or accessibility navigation service. Follow posted pedestrian routes, campus guidance, and your own judgment while walking.

Development roadmap

A campus-ready guide, built in deliberate stages.

ClioVision is moving from a working Computer Science walking pilot to a reliable, hands-free guide for the whole Missouri S&T campus. The sequence starts with real-world walking validation, then expands tours, mapping, AI, and release readiness.

  1. 01

    August 2026

    Stabilize the pilot

    Field-test routes, GPS arrivals, voice recovery, and glasses connection on real campus walks.

  2. 02

    Aug–Sep 2026

    Expand degree tours

    Curate every undergraduate program’s route, buildings, narration, timing, and field-review status.

  3. 03

    September 2026

    Build a production map

    Add sidewalk-aware mapping, building markers, accessibility choices, and offline campus support.

  4. 04

    Sep–Oct 2026

    Make guidance smarter

    Add rerouting, turn prompts, resume behavior, and hands-free commands such as “what’s next?”

  5. 05

    October 2026

    Grow campus AI

    Ground answers in official sources and make Clio aware of the current tour, stop, and visitor question.

  6. 06

    Oct 2026 onward

    Beta to release

    Harden voice and privacy, recruit testers, ship TestFlight builds, and prepare the public launch.

Next milestone

Walk the Computer Science pilot on campus and use the findings to improve arrival zones, route geometry, narration, and Bluetooth recovery before expanding to other majors.

Security, privacy, content review, and route safety are built into every phase.

Questions, answered

What to expect from the pilot.

Do I need Meta glasses?

No. The iOS pilot works on the phone; compatible Meta glasses are optional for in-ear audio. Availability depends on the phone, operating system, and connected device state.

What does “Hey Clio” do?

While the app is open and active listening is enabled, it listens for the wake phrase, captures a question, retrieves a campus answer, and reads the committed answer through the selected audio route.

Can I rely on it for walking directions?

No. Treat ClioVision as a tour companion. Routes, arrival zones, and directions are still being validated; always follow posted sidewalks, accessibility guidance, and campus rules.

What happens to voice and location data?

The app requests only the permissions needed for its features. See the plain-language privacy notice for the current pilot’s data-use overview.

Is Android available?

The active glasses and walking pilot is currently focused on iOS. Android support is on the roadmap and is not yet presented as available.

How can a university participate?

Use the partnership interest form below. We are looking for feedback on tour content, routes, visitor needs, and practical pilot opportunities.
