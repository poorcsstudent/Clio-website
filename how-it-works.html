A pilot interaction

Pick a path. Start walking. Ask naturally.

The current flow is built around a simple idea: keep the tour in front of you, and keep answers available in your ear and on the screen.

01Pair and prepare. Enter the private pairing code and connect compatible glasses if you want in-ear audio.
02Start a tour. Open the current campus walk and review the next destination in the map.
03Walk to the next stop. Follow the tour aid and monitor the GPS arrival state; use campus signage and pedestrian guidance as the authority.
04Ask Clio. Use the microphone or say “Hey Clio” while active listening is enabled and the app is open.
05Read or hear the response. The answer is shown in the app and native iOS speech reads that same text through the selected output route.
06Continue the conversation. When speech completes, the pilot returns to a ready state for the next question.
