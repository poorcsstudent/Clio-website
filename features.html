Current capabilities

Campus context, in the moment.

The current iOS pilot is deliberately focused: answer a question, guide a walk, recognize arrival, and recover cleanly between interactions.

◎

AI-guided tours

Pick a campus experience and use ClioVision to surface context while you walk between stops.

✦

Voice questions

Use the “Hey Clio” wake phrase while the app is open, then ask a natural campus question.

▣

Walking awareness

Track the current route, next stop, and arrival state through the interactive campus map.

◌

In-ear response

When compatible glasses are connected, iOS can route the spoken answer through the selected audio output.

⌖

Answer stays visible

The same answer sent to speech remains on screen so you can reread or replay it.

✓

Secure pairing

A private pairing flow establishes the current device session before campus questions are sent.
